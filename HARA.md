# Hazard Analysis and Risk Assessment (HARA)

The ISO 26262 part 3 process for analysing vehicle hazards has been used for the conventional hydraulic braking (CHB) system. The hazard severity (S), Exposure (E) and Controllability (C) classification is the basis for how the hazard was rated.

## Synthesized Hazard List and ASIL Ratings

| ID | Potential Hazard | Description | S | E | C | ASIL |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **H3** | Loss of Lateral Motion Control | Front wheels lock up; loss of steerability. | S3 | E4 | C3 | **D** |
| **H4** | Unintended Vehicle Deceleration | Deceleration exceeds command (e.g., unintended braking). | S3 | E4 | C3 | **D** |
| **H5** | Insufficient Vehicle Deceleration | Deceleration is less than commanded by the driver. | S3 | E4 | C3 | **D** |
| **H6** | Loss of Longitudinal Control | Total failure to respond to braking inputs. | S3 | E4 | C3 | **D** |
| **H1** | Unintended Lateral Motion/Yaw | Vehicle yaws more than intended without wheel lock-up. | S3 | E2 | C3 | **B** |



## Operational Scenarios
The ASIL D rating was based on 201 operating scenarios that included the following:

* A vehicle travelling at high speed (greater than 100 kph) in environments that have many vehicles, on winding roads and in slippery (i.e. wet/snow) conditions.
* In cases that have a controllability (C3) rating; where the driver would have a great deal of difficulty or not at all controlling a malfunction (e.g. unintended braking).
